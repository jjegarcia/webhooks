Practice gives immediate feedback. Ninja and Battle show blue dots for submitted
guesses and reveal results when time runs out, all hooks on the selected side are found,
or Stop round is pressed. At the end, the hook board lists correct words for that side,
marking found and missed answers.
Keyboard dots appear on the left for before-root guesses and on the right for
after-root guesses; both remain visible when switching sides.
At the end of timed rounds, gray dots mark correct hooks that were not guessed.
Stop round ends play immediately and reveals the results. In Ninja, use the
before/after marker to choose a side before starting; it remains locked during the round.
After a round, select New round
to prepare the next one and choose its side. A side without hooks cannot be started.
In Practice, use Switch side to guess before or after the root.
Battle chooses a random side, root length, and playable root for each new round.
Only the selected side is played and counted; the root picker is hidden.
Spanish uses the Spanish hook tables and treats CH, LL, Ñ, and RR as single
tiles. Physical keys 1–4 select them, and Ñ can also be typed directly.
Switching languages starts a fresh round and clears the current score and progress.
The selected language is saved in the browser and restored on the next visit.
