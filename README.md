App: https://jjegarcia.github.io/webhooks/

Practice gives immediate feedback. Ninja and Battle show blue dots for submitted
guesses and reveal results only when time runs out or all hooks are found.
At the end, the hook board lists every correct word, marking found and missed answers.
Keyboard dots appear on the left for before-root guesses and on the right for
after-root guesses; both remain visible when switching sides.
At the end of timed rounds, gray dots mark correct hooks that were not guessed.
Pausing does not reveal results; use Switch side to guess before or after the root.
